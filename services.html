Ourservices

Advisory, governance and certification across six practice areas.

What we do

Family Offices

For single or multi-family offices: estate, investments, inheritance, zakat and charitable activities managed according to Islamic principles.

Capital Market

Novel Islamic financial structures for capital market activity, designed with you and certified by our board.

Payment Solutions

We resolve Sharia banking issues and rebuild operational frameworks, including Sharia governance, audit and control functions.

Funding Companies

Financial expertise and scientific research to align product structures and certifications with each opportunity.

Dispute Resolution

Mediation and legal assistance for resolving disputes within the framework of Islamic law, aiming for peaceful outcomes.

Halal Certification

Guidance and support for obtaining Halal certification for products and businesses under Islamic dietary law.

How we work

  • Sharia Supervisory Board

    An independent board of scholars reviews your products, contracts and policies and issues the resolutions you operate under.

  • Internal Sharia Audit

    Periodic reviews of live transactions against the board's resolutions, with findings and corrective actions documented for management.

  • Express Certification

    A focused review path for a single product or contract when you need a certificate on a tight timeline.
