Certificates

We issue certificates for business contracts, property transactions and other legal matters. Each is prepared by our legal team and checked against the applicable law.

Certificate of Legal Compliance

An official document verifying that a transaction, agreement or business practice follows the applicable laws and regulations, and that the parties have met their legal obligations.

Certificate of Contract Validity

Confirms that a contract is legal and enforceable. Our team examines each clause and confirms compliance with applicable law before the certificate is issued.
