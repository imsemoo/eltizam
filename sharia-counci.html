Our ShariaCouncil

Clear, well-grounded opinions on matters governed by Sharia law.

The work of the council

Our Sharia Council offers guidance and consultation on family law, inheritance, marriage, business transactions and Islamic finance, and issues opinions based on Islamic principles.

Council members are skilled in mediation and alternative dispute resolution and aim for amicable outcomes. We work with integrity, professionalism and confidentiality.

Where the council advises

Marriage and divorce

Guidance and support on marriage and divorce matters, grounded in Islamic jurisprudence and handled in confidence.

Islamic finance

Islamic finance is governed by Sharia law. The council reviews structures, contracts and products and issues opinions on their compliance.
